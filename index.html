  • Future Factory Floor Operations



                

    Future Factory Floor Operations

    Problem Statement

    Automated factory assembly lines are creating bottle necks leading to anomalies affecting production performance
    and increased human intervention.

    Business Requirements and Goals

    Business Need:

    The company wanted to solve the problem to increase sales without affecting retention.

    Requirements:

    • Lack of real time updates to track the shop floor performance

    • Ambiguity related to bottlenecks

    • Absence of an efficient task delegation mechanism

    • Research on how competitors are solving this challenge

    Persona :

    Findings based on interviews/workshop that were conducted.


    Customer Journey

    To understand how customers find and interact with the service we created a Customer Journey Map.

    Guiding Questions:

    • What were the steps of the customer journey?

    • At what point did the user realised he needs help?

    • What were the main touchpoints at each step and what are his emotions involved?

    • What were the main pain points for the user during the customer journey?

    • What solutions did you find to treat these pain points?

    • What new features or design changes came from mapping the customer journey?


    Design Direction

    We agreed that the most important task was to increase the retention rate since it was the
    product's most important KPI.

    Guiding Questions:

    • Does the design affect any existing process?

    • Is our design solving the problem defined?

    • What have you changed in the design to this end?

    • Do we foresee any new issues due to new design changes?

    Resulting Pointers:

    • Real time updates to track the shop floor performance

    • Clear identification and intimation of the bottlenecks

    • Lucid task delegation

  • Traffic Violation Application


                

    Traffic Violation Application

    Case Study

    The Product

    Spot violations using mobile application OCR technology to report them to police. Helping in immediate
    clearance of violations.

    Problem Statement:

    Inspectors find it tedious to reduce increasing traffic violations in spotting and filling them
    on all roads and collect charges from public.

    People who spot violations are getting into arguments not able to report to police on immediately.

    Requirements:

    • Violation Detection (helmet detection)
    • Highlight violators with vehicle details (vehicle type)
    • Ability to capture number plates
    • Place to validate violation and generate tickets by comparing with online information (vehicle type, etc.)
    • Possible to fetch reports for analysis locations of increasing violation and time range for the event occurrence
    • Immediate notifications and auto allotting nearby traffic police to clear the vehicles and collecting the charges

    The Project:

    A single mobile application which can be used by both public and traffic police. Public. can report violations to police
    and get rewarded. Police gets all violations in their mobile application, all he have to do is validate and approve them.

    User Flow:

    Before I started any research, I build a user flow for current process how violations are being handled. My goal here is
    to minimise the efforts put in by traffic police inspectors to spotting and filing violations
    by involving the general public.



    Personas


    Traffic Police Inspector Persona

    James, Bangalore south

    Based on the interviews/workshop we set up with two personas. We referred to them throughout
    the entire product development process.


    General Public Persona

    Nagarajan, Bangalore

    Based on user interviews we set up with a single personas. Interview started with the way
    city is performing to what steps are necessary to solve the problem.


    Field Studies and Surveys


    I conducted field studies and sent out survey forms to collect insights.

    Guiding Questions:
    • What environments user are working in?
    • What devices are used by them massively?
    • What is causing pressure at work?
    • What frustrates them the most?
    • What are the biggest problem to solve here?
    • How the collected insights influence the design decisions?

    Paper Sketches:


    After completing my initial research, I sketch ideas. This allows for many leading iterations through varying design options.


    Visual Design

    First impression can be stolen by Visual Design. My findings during the interviews and research phase clearly
    showed what are user expectations and keeping that in mind experience and visual designs were developed.
